# Independence declaration (authors, labellers and adjudicators)

The coordinator keeps each signed copy outside the repository. The repository records only your pseudonymous code (for example `A03`) and your answers, in the participant registry.

By signing, you confirm:

1. **No involvement in the system.** You have not taken part in designing, implementing, tuning or testing the SevaFund verification system or its evaluation benchmarks.
2. **No exposure.** You have not seen:
   - its source code;
   - its rule, keyword or lexicon lists;
   - any earlier test or benchmark data set.
3. **One role only.** You act in a single role (author, labeller or adjudicator), and you will not discuss cases with participants in other roles.
4. **No private data.** You will not use any real person's private data. Invented names, phone numbers and account numbers only.
5. **AI assistance disclosed.** If any language model helped you draft a case, you mark that case `llm_assisted: true`.

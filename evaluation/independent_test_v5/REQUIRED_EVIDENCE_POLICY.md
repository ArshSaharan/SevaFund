# SevaFund documentation policy (shared with authors and labellers)

This is the platform's published policy on what a campaign must provide before it can go live. Real campaigners would see it. It says nothing about how the platform checks documents.

**Codes.** Labellers use the codes in brackets to name missing items.

## Every campaign

1. **[ID] Who benefits.** Evidence identifying the beneficiary:
   - for a person: a document from an institution naming them;
   - for an organisation: its registration (society, trust or Section 8 company), current and naming the authorised signatory.
2. **[NEED] Why the money is needed.** Evidence from the relevant institution that the need is real and current:
   - **Medical:** a diagnosis, treatment plan, admission record or doctor's note naming the treatment.
   - **Education:** enrolment or admission confirmation for the stated course and year.
   - **Community:** a project description, plus evidence that the organisation is responsible for it.
3. **[COST] How much is needed.** A cost document that:
   - is issued by the party that will be paid, such as a hospital, institution or supplier;
   - identifies that party by name and address or contact;
   - is dated, and carries a reference number or a signatory;
   - is current: issued within the last 12 months, or within the validity period it states;
   - states an amount that covers the amount requested. If insurance, a government scheme, a scholarship or an advance covers part of the cost, the document or the campaign must state the remaining balance.

   **Examples:** an estimate, quotation, bill, fee demand, invoice or work order. A letter confirming enrolment or need does **not** establish the amount.

   **No formal quotation?** A campaigner who cannot obtain one may say so. The platform will then help obtain the amount directly from the institution. The campaign cannot go live until the amount is established in one of these ways.
4. **[PAYEE] Where the money goes.** Payout details:
   - an account in the name of the institution or supplier;
   - or an account in the name of the verified beneficiary.

## Before a campaign goes live

The platform independently contacts:
- the beneficiary;
- the institution or registering authority;
- the payee.

It uses contact details from official sources, not those printed in the campaign. A campaign that has every document above may still wait until these contacts confirm it.
